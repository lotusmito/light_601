                 On behalf of Ling Shen Ching Tze Temple, also known as Lei Zang Temple, we are truly honored

            the bridge between cultures and histories.

            November 14, 1997—more than 28 years ago—when our temple donated a collection of books written by
            our founder, Grandmaster Sheng-Yen Lu, to the Redmond Library.  Today, as I stand here in this very
